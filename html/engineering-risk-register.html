i

Scoring guide

Apply the same definitions consistently.

Likelihood

  1. Rare — exceptional circumstances
  2. Unlikely — not expected, but possible
  3. Possible — may occur
  4. Likely — expected in many circumstances
  5. Almost certain — expected to occur

Impact

  1. Negligible — minimal disruption
  2. Minor — localised, manageable effect
  3. Moderate — meaningful delivery or service impact
  4. Major — significant customer, delivery or operational impact
  5. Severe — critical business, customer, security or compliance impact

Ex

Example risk statement

Specific and actionable.

Dependency · Authentication integration Because the external identity provider has not confirmed its interface change, authentication integration may miss the release window, potentially delaying customer onboarding.

Avoid vague entries such as “Integration risk: amber.” State what may happen, why it may happen, and the consequence.
