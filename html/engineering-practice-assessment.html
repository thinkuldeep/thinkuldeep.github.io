03

Assessment guidance

Apply consistent interpretation across teams.

Pass

The control exists, operates on the required scope, and evidence demonstrates that it works.

Partial

The control exists or works inconsistently, has incomplete coverage, or needs material improvement.

Fail

The control is absent, not operating, or cannot demonstrate the required assurance.

Severity

Rate the impact and urgency of the gap using agreed organisational definitions.

Exception approval

Record the authorised approver and reference, plus any expiry or review date in the evidence notes.

Retest

Verify remediation using fresh evidence. A retest pass should not be inferred from a change being marked complete.
