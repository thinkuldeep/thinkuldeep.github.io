✓ Rules for good weekly reporting

  1. Report outcomes and exceptions, not a diary of activities.
  2. Include changes from the previous reporting period.
  3. Every amber or red status explains why, what happens next, and who owns the response.
  4. Distinguish committed dates from forecasts and estimates.
  5. Link to source systems instead of duplicating detailed technical evidence.
  6. Avoid misleading completion percentages when remaining work or uncertainty is significant.
  7. Treat a missing update as a visibility problem—not proof that a team is healthy or unhealthy.
