Minimum information for each material risk

Record enough context to support decisions, track mitigation and understand the remaining exposure.

1
Risk and impactDescribe the event, affected outcome and potential consequences.
2
Likelihood and severityUse defined scales and assess the risk consistently.
3
Ownership and responseName one accountable owner; document mitigation, contingency and residual risk.
4
Due date and evidenceTrack target dates, escalation thresholds, supporting evidence and related decisions.

Consistent risk assessment

Assess likelihood and impact using organisation-wide definitions, then determine the required response.

LikelihoodRare · Possible · Likely
ImpactModerate · Major · Severe
ResponseMonitor · Mitigate · Escalate
Do not average away critical risks. A critical security or safety concern requires explicit assessment and action, regardless of the scores of unrelated risks.
